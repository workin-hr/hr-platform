# Production Behavior Evidence

## Behavior

Record the observed business or system behavior in neutral, factual terms.
Avoid explaining it as a design choice unless evidence supports that claim.

## Source Of Evidence

Identify where the behavior was seen, such as:

- PHP source or template logic
- SQL procedure or trigger
- runtime log or captured response
- support note
- operator demonstration
- customer-reported behavior reproduced by the team

## Confidence

Use an explicit confidence label such as:

- observed directly
- strongly evidenced
- plausible but incomplete
- low confidence / needs confirmation

Do not treat low-confidence evidence as settled fact.

## Notes

Capture ambiguity, contradictions, environment assumptions, or downstream
areas that depend on this behavior staying correct during migration.
