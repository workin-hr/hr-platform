# Non-Functional Requirements

## Requirement

Record the quality attribute or operating expectation, such as correctness,
performance, auditability, availability, tenant isolation, or supportability.

## Why It Matters

Describe the business or operational reason this requirement matters. Tie it to
customer workflows, compliance, migration safety, or delivery constraints where
possible.

## Evidence

Link the source that justifies the requirement, such as stakeholder input,
legacy behavior, contract commitment, incident history, or architecture
direction.

## Candidate Measurement

Describe how the requirement might eventually be measured. If a numeric target
is not yet justified, record the measurement approach without inventing a
threshold.

## Open Questions

Record what still needs clarification before the requirement can drive design
or acceptance criteria.
