# Customer Impact Analysis

## Change Candidate

Record the proposed change, migration step, contract evolution, or workflow
adjustment being analyzed.

## Affected Customers

Identify which customer groups, tenant types, employee roles, or
administrative users are likely to notice the change.

## Compatibility Risk

Describe how existing workflows, data expectations, client behavior, or
integration assumptions could break.

## Operational Risk

Describe rollout, support, communication, training, or migration risks that
could affect adoption even if the feature is technically correct.

## Mitigation Notes

Capture the current best mitigation, such as staged rollout, compatibility
shim, customer notice, operator training, or explicit deferral until more
evidence exists.
