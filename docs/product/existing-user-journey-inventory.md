# Existing User Journey Inventory

## Journey

Record the business journey or workflow in user-facing language.

## Primary Actor

Identify who performs the journey, such as employee, manager, HR
administrator, payroll operator, or device/site administrator.

## Trigger

Describe what starts the journey: scheduled event, user action, approval step,
device event, or external dependency.

## Current Steps

Capture the steps as actually observed, not as the team assumes they should
work.

## Business Rules

List the business rules, validations, approvals, or exception handling that
shape the journey.

## Evidence

Link the evidence supporting the journey, such as screenshots, operator demo,
legacy code, support notes, or runtime traces.

## Risk If Changed

Describe the business, support, or compatibility consequence of changing the
journey incorrectly.
